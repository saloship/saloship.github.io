★ A method I actually use

The Cold-Start Audit

Sometimes I get handed something no one has audited before — no template, no map. I kept taking the same path through it, so I gave it a name.

01

Map the actuals

What really happens, not what the document says.

→
02

Find the hand-offs

Risk hides at the seams and decision points.

→
03

Risks, bottom-up

Built from the process, not a checklist.

→
04

Pressure-test

Walkthroughs to check the map is true.

→
05

Controls & proof

What good looks like, and what evidences it.

→
06

Leave a template

So the next person starts warm, not cold.

Step six is the one I care about most — turning a one-off into something reusable. It's how an ISO 27001 policy build became a review structure I reused, and how a set of testing templates ended up shared around the team.
