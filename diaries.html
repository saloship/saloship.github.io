01Macleodganj · July 2024

Macleodganj

Camping in the mountains will always be my favourite kind of trip, and Triund is a good spot for it. The trek was easy — grasslands and rock, plenty of viewpoints, and majestic mountains sitting in the clouds in the distance. Dharamkot's market needs a full day; it has a whole vibe of its own. We had less time for Dharamshala, but its market and monastery are a different cultural experience.

An easy trail where you can find your own path.

Monastery, stadium and tea gardens.

02Kasol · July 2022

Kasol

Parvati Valley is the kind of place that slows you right down — riverside cafes, pine air, and the hot springs at Manikaran. The Kheerganga trek rewards you with a hot spring at the very top; go slow and let the valley set the pace.

03Jibhi · March 2024

Jibhi

I went in late March, so it was cold — but only at night, perfect to explore by day and sit around a bonfire in the evening. Day two rained hard, so we mostly cafe-hopped, but we got the feeling of being above the clouds; the Serolsar trek was under fresh snow. Two nights covers the main spots — stretch to four to do all the treks without rushing.

A small trek with a river and mountains on one side.

04Chopta · May 2024

Chopta

We stayed near the start of the Tungnath trek, so mornings were easy — just start walking. The path is well paved, with straight shortcuts to jump. It's easy to reach Chandrashila and back the same day, though some stay at Tungnath for the sunrise. Deoriatal is pleasant too — forest, cliff-edge viewpoints, and a calm emerald lake. Uttarakhand is called Dev Bhumi, and it truly felt like a divine memory.

Tungnath Trek 3680 m

Highest Shiva temple in the world, on to Chandrashila.

Deoriatal Lake 2438 m

Emerald lake amid dense rhododendron forest.

On the bank of the Alaknanda river.

05Jaipur · January 2024

Jaipur

We had 2.5 days — enough to see everything and still shop at the end. The markets are beautiful for clothes, bags, spices, everything, and mostly within 5–10 km, so easy to start the day. Winter is the best time; the city opens late and closes early. Timing is everything with the temples — they close early and sometimes have lines.

Palaces

City Palace, Hawa Mahal, Amber & Jal Mahal.

Chokhi Dhani

Rajasthan on a plate, and a whole cultural set.

Museums

Albert Hall, the Wax Museum, gem & jewel.

Forts & gates

Nahargarh, Jaigarh, Patrika Gate.

06Kerala · March 2022

Kerala

Munnar is all tea gardens and postcard sunsets, with Kochi's spice markets and backwaters to round it out. The boat ride at Mattupetty — hills mirrored in still water — is the calm I remember most.
